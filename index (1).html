How the two experiences connect

Observe → Understand → Act. SmogGuide illustrates why communities need environmental observations and practical low-emission decisions. MissionCraft demonstrates the constraints engineers face when designing an observation satellite. Its NASA POWER solar dataset is historical educational context—not live pollution monitoring or satellite telemetry. SmogGuide's Open-Meteo data are modeled forecasts, not readings from a MissionCraft satellite.

Try the SmogGuide air-quality plan, then build an Earth-observation satellite in MissionCraft. Compare the value of environmental information with the design trade-offs required to collect it.
